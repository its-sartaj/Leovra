Frequently Asked Questions — Leovra Enterprises

Is Cash on Delivery (COD) available on Leovra Enterprises?

Yes, Cash on Delivery (COD) is available on all products across India. You can also pay via UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm) for instant confirmation.

What is the return policy for Leovra Enterprises?

Leovra offers a 3-day return window from the date of delivery for defective, damaged, or wrong products received. Correctly delivered items in good condition are not eligible for return.

How long does delivery take?

Orders are dispatched via Shiprocket Express courier partners (Delhivery, Blue Dart, Shadowfax) within 24-48 hours. Delivery usually takes 2-5 business days depending on your pincode.
